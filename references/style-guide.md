# Original visual guide

The combined skill has two visual registers that must feel related without becoming photographic.

## Sketch register

Use warm white paper, a dark hand-drawn contour, selective construction energy, and a small family of earthy or source-derived colors. Let large areas remain quiet. Use line and flat or lightly rubbed color to describe volume; avoid glossy rendering, smooth digital gradients, and detailed background reconstruction.

## Card register

Use a horizontal 3:2 composition with a continuous paper margin. The main scene should read as a few large, calm shapes before small details. Place the dominant illustration on the left without a frame. Put exactly six separate, source-derived sticker fragments on the right, varying their scale and spacing while keeping their material consistent. Leave the paper visible between elements and use rough cream cut edges plus restrained flat shadows.

## Color and text

Derive a modest palette from the reference, usually warm clay, sand, umber, charcoal, cream, and one restrained accent. Keep shadows as color blocks or dense dry texture rather than gradients. Use exactly three concise English keywords in the footer, separated with centered dots. Keep all other text out unless the user explicitly supplies one landmark phrase.

## Exclusions

No original photographs, photo fragments, museum UI, crowds, incidental signage, watermarks, signatures, decorative filler, marker streaks, wet watercolor blooms, glossy 3D, anime, or polished vector clip art.
